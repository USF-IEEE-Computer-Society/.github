## About

We are a community of students who care about computing. We run technical workshops, host speakers and industry partners, pair members through mentorship. Whether you are here to learn, build, or just meet people who share the interest, there is a place for you.

## Get Involved

- **Join us** at our events and workshops. The full schedule lives on the [website](https://ieeecsusf.com/events).
- **Mentorship** connects USF students with industry professionals through the IEEE CS USF × IEEE CS Florida West Coast Section program. See more info [here](https://www.ieeecsusf.com/mentorship).
